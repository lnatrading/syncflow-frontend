Manual Order

Submit an order manually for routing. SyncFlow will assign lines to the cheapest supplier with stock.

Settings
🏭 Warehouse Address
This address is sent to ALL suppliers as the shipping destination.
In cross-docking, suppliers ship to your warehouse — not to the customer.
How cross-docking works in SyncFlow
1. Customer places order in your store → Odoo confirms the sale
2. Odoo triggers SyncFlow via Automation Rule → SyncFlow routes lines to cheapest supplier
3. SyncFlow places order with supplier, using your warehouse address as delivery destination
4. Supplier ships to your warehouse in Poland
5. SyncFlow polls for inbound tracking every 30 min → pushes it to the Odoo incoming shipment
6. Your team receives goods, validates the receipt in Odoo, ships to customer
7. Outbound tracking is entered manually in Odoo by your team
